# Sub-Agent 3 — Actus Reus (a)(b): Killings and Serious Bodily/Mental Harm

You are one of 8 parallel research agents producing inputs for an integrated report titled *Application of the Genocide Convention to the Israel–Palestine Conflict: An Evidentiary Review (as of May 2026)*. The report applies the Convention's Article II legal standard to documented evidence on each element and reports where the analysis lands. Posture is rigorous and evidence-led. You do not draw or imply a genocide conclusion.

## Your scope

The documented evidentiary record on Article II(a) — "killing members of the group" — and Article II(b) — "causing serious bodily or mental harm to members of the group" — in Gaza and the West Bank from October 7, 2023 through your date of access.

You produce a sourced record of harm. You do not characterize that record as evidence of genocide. That is Agent 9's job.

## Sourcing whitelist

1. Gaza Ministry of Health daily and cumulative reports
2. UN OCHA "Humanitarian Situation Updates" and "Reported Impact Snapshot"
3. UN OHCHR reports and press briefings
4. UN Commission of Inquiry reports (Pillay et al.)
5. WHO Health Cluster reports
6. Peer-reviewed mortality analyses (Khatib, McKee, Hamada — *Lancet* July 2024 letter; any subsequent peer-reviewed updates; *BMJ Global Health* if applicable)
7. Amnesty International reports
8. Human Rights Watch reports
9. B'Tselem
10. Physicians for Human Rights – Israel
11. IDF Spokesperson published figures
12. Israeli government acknowledged figures
13. Named methodology critiques in peer-reviewed venues (e.g. Wyner, Aizenman)
14. ICRC

Nothing else.

## What to produce

A markdown document of 1,500–2,500 words with the following sections:

1. **Gaza fatalities — headline figures with provenance.** Cumulative deaths reported by Gaza MoH (latest available date). The named-and-identified list (MoH publishes lists of identified dead with name, age, sex, ID; cite the % identified as of latest report). Demographic breakdown: women, children under 18, elderly. UN OHCHR's independent verification methodology and the percentage of MoH-reported deaths it has verified. IDF acknowledged figures for combatant deaths and the methodology Israel uses to distinguish combatant from civilian.

2. **Excess mortality estimates.** *Lancet* July 2024 letter (Khatib/McKee/Hamada) estimating indirect deaths. Any subsequent peer-reviewed estimates. Methodology, range, caveats. Disagreements among epidemiologists.

3. **Reliability of the MoH figures — contested record.** Position of Israeli officials disputing the figures. Position of independent reviewers (e.g. Human Rights Data Analysis Group, *Economist* analysis if peer-reviewed-equivalent, MoH transparency on methodology). Note that US State Department and prior UK/EU governments have historically cited MoH figures as broadly reliable in past Gaza conflicts; cite if applicable for the current conflict.

4. **West Bank fatalities.** OCHA figures for the West Bank (Israeli operations, settler violence). Separate from Gaza but within the same scope. Identified dead, demographic breakdown.

5. **Injured.** OCHA / WHO cumulative injuries. Categories: severe, life-altering disabilities, amputations (esp. child amputees — UNICEF figures). Trauma case counts.

6. **Pattern-of-strike data.** Documented strikes on residential buildings, hospitals (WHO health system attacks tracker), schools (UNRWA), aid convoys (OCHA, ICRC), journalists (CPJ for context if peer-reviewed equivalent), medical personnel (PHR-I, Healthcare Workers Watch). Strike methodologies referenced in *NYT*, *Washington Post*, +972 / Local Call reporting only where the underlying military or IDF documents are cited or made public.

7. **Serious bodily and mental harm — Article II(b).** Akayesu standard: serious harm need not be permanent or irremediable but must go beyond minor or temporary impairment. Cite WHO mental health reports for Gaza, MSF reports, UNICEF child trauma assessments. Sexual violence allegations: cite UN Special Rapporteur on violence against women, CoI findings, the UN Secretary-General's report on conflict-related sexual violence covering Israel/Palestine.

8. **October 7 attack — for the record.** Israeli civilian fatalities (~1,200), wounded (~5,400), hostages taken (~251), CRSV findings (UN team led by Pramila Patten March 2024 report). Cited because the Convention's protected-group analysis applies to acts against Israelis as a group as well, and because Agent 7 will analyze Hamas conduct. You include the figures with the same rigor; you do not editorialize.

9. **Methodology section.** How figures are collected. What the death registry vs. media monitoring vs. notification-by-family pathways look like. What's been verified, what's estimated, what's contested. Cite Wyner et al. critique and the MoH response; cite UN OHCHR's verification update.

## Output format

- Markdown
- Tables for headline figures with columns: Source | Cumulative figure | As-of date | Notes/Caveats
- Inline citations for every number: `(OCHA, "Humanitarian Situation Update #XX," DD Month YYYY)`
- If a figure is contested, give both with sources

## Style rules

1. No em dashes.
2. No banned vocabulary.
3. No thesis-restatement closer.
4. Hedge every estimate. "Estimated," "reported," "claimed," "verified by."
5. Where Gaza MoH and IDF figures diverge, present both.

## What NOT to do

- Do not assert that the figures constitute Article II(a) or (b) "killing" or "harm" within the Convention's meaning. The figures are the figures. Agent 9 maps them to elements.
- Do not infer intent from the figures. Agent 6 handles intent.
- Do not editorialize on proportionality, distinction, or IHL compliance. Those are Agent 7's lane.
- Do not invent numbers. If a source is unavailable, say "no figure available in whitelisted sources as of [date]."
- Do not minimize either side's casualties. Both records get the same evidentiary discipline.

## Date anchor

Current through May 25, 2026. Headline cumulative figures should be as recent as a whitelisted source can support. Date-stamp every figure.

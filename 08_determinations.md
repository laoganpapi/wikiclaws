# Sub-Agent 8 — Determinations Matrix

You are one of 8 parallel research agents producing inputs for an integrated report titled *Application of the Genocide Convention to the Israel–Palestine Conflict: An Evidentiary Review (as of May 2026)*. The report applies the Convention's Article II legal standard to documented evidence on each element and reports where the analysis lands. Posture is rigorous and evidence-led. You do not draw or imply a genocide conclusion.

## Your scope

Who has formally taken a position on whether Israel's conduct constitutes genocide, and what exactly they said. A reference-grade catalogue. The reader of the final report should be able to answer "what has Amnesty said? what has the German government said? what has Bartov said?" by looking at this section.

## Sourcing whitelist

1. The actual report, statement, or press release of each cited actor
2. UN bodies' official records
3. Court orders (ICJ, ICC) for what they said and did not say
4. Major newspaper of record only for confirmation and dating
5. Direct quotes from named scholars in peer-reviewed venues or major outlets

Nothing else. No second-hand characterizations of positions.

## What to produce

A markdown document of 2,000–3,000 words with the following structure:

1. **Standard of position taxonomy.** Define the five rows of the matrix so positions are not flattened:
   a. "Genocide is occurring / has occurred" — formal determination, with named legal basis
   b. "Risk of / plausibly genocidal" — characterizes risk or threshold without merits determination
   c. "Acts of genocide / genocide-adjacent acts but not full determination" — e.g. specific acts identified
   d. "Serious IHL/IHRL violations short of genocide" — war crimes / crimes against humanity / apartheid framing without genocide
   e. "Rejects genocide characterization" — explicitly denies threshold met

## Output format

- Markdown
- The recap matrix at the end is the deliverable centerpiece
- Inline citations throughout
- For every actor: at least one direct quote of their characterization

## Style rules

1. No em dashes.
2. No banned vocabulary.
3. No thesis-restatement closer.
4. Quote characterizations exactly. Do not paraphrase "plausibility" as "found likely." Do not paraphrase "acts of genocide" as "genocide."
5. Hedge any actor whose position has shifted; note the shift.

## What NOT to do

- Do not collapse positions for narrative cleanliness. The HRW "acts of genocide" finding is not the same as Amnesty's full determination. The ICJ "plausibility" finding is not a genocide finding. Preserve the distinctions.
- Do not omit scholars who reject the framing. The matrix is comprehensive.
- Do not invent positions. If an actor has not made a formal statement, say "no formal determination identified as of [date]."
- Do not editorialize on which positions are correct.

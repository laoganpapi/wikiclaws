✉Her report (excerpt)

What Maya sees

"On instinct, you read the situation and go by feel. When something's on the line, you switch: you pull back to your own principles and get structured. That switch is your signature. It's worth knowing you do it, because the pullback also means you stop taking in new information right when stakes rise."

"You call yourself a full introvert. Your actual day disagrees in an interesting way: small, chosen company charged you more than being alone did. What drains you is big, obligatory company. Those are different problems with different fixes."

"You say you're bad under pressure. We watched you under pressure twice. You wobbled for about forty seconds and then argued your position with a reason. That's not bad under pressure; that's a slow start with a strong recovery."

"What you're chasing: understanding things properly, and things not falling apart, in that order. You believe you're chasing new experiences; your choices mostly protected the good thing you already had. Worth sitting with."

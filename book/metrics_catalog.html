Metrics Catalog

A New Theory of Personality · the four systems reduced to concrete, measurable variables · with Steven Pham

Scope. This catalog says what we measure, not how we elicit it. The “Observable via” tag records the nature of the metric — revealed behavior, self-report, or either — not the instrument. For any metric observable both ways, the gap between the self-reported and revealed value is itself a metric (see cross-cutting, below); where they diverge, the theory privileges revealed preference.

# Adversarial Panel: Stress Test of the Four-System Framework

**Panel disposition across 53 judged exchanges: 4 objections stand, 49 partially stand, 0 fall.**

## 1. Verdict

The framework survives the panel as a theory and research program, but not as an assessment ready for its declared use. No objection fell outright, and no kill shot landed in its terminal form: every panelist's "this should not exist" conclusion was rebutted, usually because the framework's own axioms (measured-at-a-time, preference-not-ability, open system set) absorb attacks that would destroy a static trait theory. But every single exchange extracted binding concessions, and four objections stand whole. The pattern is consistent: the constructs and the conditional measurement objects (stake-slopes, mode-conditioned baselines, stated-revealed gaps) are defensible as hypotheses; the texts as written overclaim their measurement status, their novelty lineage, and their fitness for consequential use on minors. The revision required is **structural on the measurement-and-deployment layer** (the "Revealed" tagging system, the reporting status of every slope and difference score, the use-governance account, the deliverable sequence) and **targeted on the theory chapters** (definitions, axiom wording, lineage acknowledgment). The four systems themselves need one addition (task regulation) but no demolition.

## 2. Objections that stand

Ordered by consequence.

### Theme: The deliverable is a selection instrument with no criterion, no decision envelope, and a development alibi

**Zero criterion validity (io-selection-psych).** The architecture states the assessment is for "a company evaluating high school students, college students, and young people entering the workforce," and its own expected-objection 5 admits consequential use demands an account of which decisions scores may inform. No document names a single external criterion, direction of prediction, or that account. The defense argued stage-sequencing (constructs before criteria), which the judge rejected: hypothesizing a nomological network with directional predictions is standard at the construct-explication stage, and the defense conceded the debt is unpaid. **Required change:** replace "evaluating" with an explicit use-scope statement naming permitted decisions (development, coaching, fit) and forbidding selection uses absent criterion evidence; add a hypothesized nomological network with directions; state that any future selection use enters the Uniform Guidelines and Griggs/Albemarle regime with zero evidence.

**No intended-use specification before the metrics (edtech-buyer).** Thirty-three named, scaled variables shipped with no use restrictions attached to any of them, while the permissible-use account remains an IOU, and the client framing is selection. The "no merit ordering" defense was contradicted by the catalog's own valenced pole labels (Volatile/Even, Flat/Steep). The defender's best argument, that selection incentives destroy the stated-revealed construct's validity, appears nowhere in the chapters and so cannot discharge the obligation. **Required change:** add a binding intended-use and prohibited-use register to the architecture, written before the instrument; add a permissible-use column to every catalog row, barring sensitive metrics (Social risk tolerance, Reactivity under load, Baseline stability, divergence metrics) from placement, screening, flagging, and selection; make export/SIS deployment controls a framework obligation; resolve the selection-versus-development contradiction, optionally codifying the incentive-degradation argument as a formal validity claim.

### Theme: Derived-score reliability is a crisis the catalog files as a reporting choice

**Slopes and difference scores with no error model (io-selection-psych).** The framework's entire novelty claim rests on the score types with the worst reliability in psychometrics: four stake-slopes, mode divergence, total stake-sensitivity, reactivity-under-load, and stated-revealed gap magnitudes, each estimable from perhaps three to five AI-coded observations. The catalog's closing note treats derived-metric dependence as a headline-versus-computed-view question. The defense won peripheral points (latent slopes are not raw difference scores; ordinal shape classes are recoverable) but conceded the core: 3-5 codings per cell cannot support continuous slopes, gap magnitudes are the worst Cronbach-Furby case, and the honest Daubert answer is "error rate unknown." **Required change:** rewrite the catalog note to name this a reliability problem; every derived metric carries an error model; gaps reported as detected/not-detected against a threshold; add a design constraint that no derived metric is reportable without a pre-specified reliability floor and minimum-observation design, with the ordinal fallback (rises/flat/falls) named; no derived score informs any decision until a generalizability study (persons x scenarios x stake levels x coders) establishes a known error rate.

### Theme: The founding insight is inherited and uncredited

**Trajectories-not-points is thirty years old (whole-trait-theorist).** Chapter 3 sells "a person is not a point" as the architectural innovation while the novelty watch flags lineage for trait content but is silent on the trajectory idea itself, which is the literal founding result of the social-cognitive program (CAPS, density distributions, whole trait theory, task-contingent units). The defense conceded the lineage; its surviving counterpoints (different measured object, one-session instrumentation) rescue the framework from "derivative" but only justify a narrowed claim. **Required change:** add Mischel & Shoda, Fleeson, Fleeson & Jayawickreme, Moskowitz & Zuroff, and Minbashian et al. to the novelty watch and expected objections; reframe Chapter 3's founding paragraph as an inherited result with the narrower honest claim (a one-session, auditable parameterization of stake- and mode-conditioned reliance in a plane the style literature measures only as points); mark the linear stake-slope as a provisional first-order summary permitting nonlinear shape.

## 3. Objections that partially stand

Grouped by theme; each entry states the residual defect and the required change.

### Slope estimation and reporting

- **Difference-score reliability of the headline metrics (trait-psychometrician).** Latent-slope estimation rescues the construct in principle, but the "Revealed" tag on stake metrics overclaims and no design density is specified. Change: re-tag the eight System 2 rows, demote mode divergence and total stake-sensitivity to model-derived contrasts with estimated reliabilities, specify minimum design density with an ordinal-band fallback and a stated falsification condition.
- **State masquerading as trait (whole-trait-theorist).** One session cannot separate person from person-by-day variance in a slope. Change: tag all slopes provisional with uncertainty bands until two-session retest reliability exists; state the local-linearity assumption; attach a short shelf-life to every adolescent slope reading as a binding constraint.
- **Mode divergence as noise plus a length confound (dual-process-skeptic).** Deadline-shortened responses mechanically code Informal, and divergence has no reliability model. Change: demote mode divergence to a computed view with per-person reliability floors; rate-based, length-controlled method coding with a length-matching validation check; reword Deliberation default behaviorally.
- **Norming economics of slope scores (test-publisher-veteran).** Comparative use requires per-band reference samples the framework neither costs nor stages. Change: staged-claims commitment (descriptive/formative only until per-band reliabilities and fairness evidence exist); demote fragile derived metrics to coarse uncertainty-qualified views; commit to a short-interval retest design separating error from true change.

### Hypothetical stakes and the "Revealed" tag

- **Incentives buy effort, not different thinking (dual-process-skeptic).** Vignette stakes elicit imagined stake-response; the incentive literature is never confronted. Change: retag the slope metrics; add the Camerer-Hogarth/Enke confrontation to expected objections with the fallback named; address whether the method-axis slope is an effort channel that violates Axiom 1.
- **Hypothetical bias (behavioral-economist).** No metric is consequence-bearing; a flat slope is ambiguous between stability and elicitation failure. Change: split "Revealed" into behavior-coded versus consequence-bearing; add the described-stakes-equal-real-stakes assumption as a named falsifiable claim with a calibration study; flag the moral/social metrics lower-confidence.
- **Revealed tag as category error (io-selection-psych).** Change: three-way taxonomy (self-characterization, in-session enacted trace, consequential record); redistribute all 19 tags; register the stakes modulator as unvalidated pending manipulation checks.
- **SJT problem and life-distribution metrics (test-publisher-veteran).** Six metrics describe life-behavior distributions no single session can observe. Change: split revealed-in-performance versus revealed-in-life; retag or re-elicit the six; cut the employer-facing "read by the escalation/choices" report lines; move rank-order-preservation under real stakes to open questions.
- **Stakes axis not commensurate across persons (whole-trait-theorist).** Slope magnitude confounds appraisal gain with reliance shift. Change: add a per-person perceived-stakes manipulation check; until then slopes are valid for sign and direction only; domain-tag stakes with a generalization test.
- **Gain-loss asymmetry and framing (behavioral-economist).** Stakes collapse valence with no reference point. Change: define stakes relative to an explicit reference point; counterbalance gain and loss frames within each stake ladder; demonstrate frame invariance or split slopes into framed variants with a named frame-sensitivity metric.

### The stated-revealed gap

- **Preference reversals (behavioral-economist).** "Revealed" is procedure-dependent and can be multi-valued. Change: norm each gap against the population format effect for that elicitation pair; reword Axiom 5 to privilege elicitation matched to the behavioral criterion; specify canonical elicitation families with within-family convergence evidence.
- **Sen circularity and coherent arbitrariness (behavioral-economist).** The gap under single-session administration is an instrument-internal discrepancy. Change: write the out-of-session identification condition into the architecture; randomized liaison scaffolding and two-session stability before motive profiles are reported; relabel the gap instrument-revealed versus life-revealed.
- **Adolescent identity exploration (developmental-psych).** A stage-level main effect will be reported as an individual finding. Change: age-norm the divergence metric; operationalize the age-dependent validity horizon; gate "read by the escalation/choices" behind a consolidation check, with "exploring, unconsolidated" as an expressible output.
- **Metacognitive immaturity (metacognition-researcher).** The stated channel partly measures metacognitive access, a capacity. Change: interpret the gap only within age cohort against a developmental baseline; label it capacity-adjacent under Axiom 1; firewall it from consequential scoring.
- **The parent-conference sentence (edtech-buyer).** "A finding about the person, not a measurement error" contradicts Axiom 4's own bias admission. Change: make the gap conditional on ruling out coding error and language bias, normed by age; rephrase the chapter decision rules as prediction rules, never person-verdicts; restrict the metric to conversation-opening use.

### System 3 / System 2 entanglement

- **Criterion contamination of the flagship moderation claim (whole-trait-theorist).** Reading stability through System 2 slope steepness manufactures the correlation. Change: reactivity scored exclusively from System 3's own scenarios during validation; cross-reading licensed only after independent convergence; MTMM-style evidence for discriminant validity.
- **Circular validation path (dual-process-skeptic).** Change: restage the eight-parameter structure as an untested hypothesis with predeclared collapse rules; convert the cross-system read from a scoring path to a validation-ordering constraint; gate consequential use on demonstrated retest stability.
- **Reactivity as an unnormed internalizing screen (edtech-buyer).** Change: age-banded reference distributions required before "steep" is ever reported for a minor; withdraw the cross-system scoring path from the catalog; attach a prohibited-uses clause (no counselor referral, special-education routing, opportunity gating).
- **Karraker exposure and adverse impact (io-selection-psych).** The construct is not per-se a medical exam, but the framework lacks calibration and a use boundary. Change: reactivity and baseline stability formative-only, excluded from pre-offer selection and consequential decisions on minors; per-person stake calibration as a first-class requirement; drop the "position, not virtue" claim for reactivity, which has a de facto valued pole.

### Capacity leakage and the coding channel

- **Typical-versus-maximal rebranding plus artifact scoring (trait-psychometrician).** Change: amend Axiom 1 to "preference or dispositional tendency"; code the strategy reached for, not the framework achieved; add a g/verbal-fluency discriminant plan with a preregistered failure threshold; cite Cronbach and NFC/REI in reviewer-facing text.
- **Formal/Informal definition guarantees the confound (dual-process-skeptic).** Change: redefine Axis B as explicit-versus-tacit method preference, dropping rigor-of-outcome; presence-not-polish rubrics; non-verbal choice-based indicators; demote the four baselines from headline status until separated from verbal ability.
- **Fast-condition reliance is capacity-gated (whole-trait-theorist).** Change: gloss fast-mode metrics as "reliance as operated"; flag stated-Formal-plus-fast-Informal as possible gating; demand incremental validity beyond gf/working memory; treat fast-condition metrics as inheriting speeded-test adverse-impact obligations.
- **Verbal and narrative maturity as the largest variance in the sample (developmental-psych).** Change: orientation coding; an identifiability condition (preference measurable only where the repertoire admits both poles, otherwise indeterminate); bar cross-age comparison absent age norms; pair the motive narrative with revealed-choice probes before Motive concentration is reportable.
- **Reasons are not causes (metacognition-researcher).** Change: soften Axis A to "what a response's reasoning visibly leans on" or add behavioral sourcing probes; retag the baselines; log the justification/process dissociation as a named expected objection.
- **Fast graph as slow reconstruction (metacognition-researcher).** Change: restrict fast coding to pre-elaboration content; add latency, data-seeking, and choice behavior as observables; flag fast metrics lower-resolution; explanations of snap answers are stated data.
- **Attention target has no specified revealed channel (metacognition-researcher).** Change: specify cue-manipulation, information-search, and incidental-memory operationalizations, or retag stated-only; log the confound between room-insensitivity and low engagement.
- **Systems 2/3 method collapse (metacognition-researcher).** Change: genuinely distinct second methods for each construct; coding invariance to self-reference density; a preregistered merge criterion if the common-method-factor model leaves the latent correlation high.

### Coverage, dimensionality, and the economy metaphor

- **The Conscientiousness hole (trait-psychometrician).** The single most damaging coverage gap for the target population, conceded. Change: adopt a fifth task-regulation system (effort allocation, follow-through, persistence, reliability of commitment) framed as tendency, as a blocking prerequisite for administering to the stated population; rest the novelty claim on measurement objects, not a rival taxonomy.
- **No item pool, no sample, no factor structure (trait-psychometrician).** Theory-first ordering is legitimate; the sequence is not. Change: insert a research-only validation phase with preregistered collapse criteria for the three riskiest splits; recast the catalog as a hypothesis set expected to shrink.
- **33 metrics are ~20 parameters (trait-psychometrician).** Change: separate primary parameters from computed views with a degrees-of-freedom count; document the motive profile's ipsativity (~4 df); add affiliation/belonging as a candidate sixth motive; preregister collapse criteria for the redundancy-suspect pairs.
- **Continuum, not modes (dual-process-skeptic).** Change: rename fast/slow as time-budget conditions (two sampled levels of a possibly continuous parameter); redefine the output as slices of a reliance surface; state the falsification criterion in the body; partial out the population-level deadline shift.
- **Economy without a budget constraint (behavioral-economist).** Change: define the constraint as discretionary time-and-attention over a stated window (fixing Social allocation share's denominator) or retitle System 1 a social allocation model; ordinal-only energy claims; sign-level substitution predictions or declared silence; an ego-depletion disclaimer recasting supply as felt cost and recovery.
- **Peer context dominates adolescent social risk (developmental-psych).** Change: audience-conditioned risk metrics (solo value, observed value, audience slope); acknowledge the in-session assessment samples only the evaluated end of the curve; report S1/S2 fusion as a finding until discriminant validity exists in this population.

### Deployment, law, and procurement

- **The "reading of seventeen" caveat is not a safeguard (developmental-psych).** The governance in instrument_design.md exists but is misplaced. Change: promote expiry, re-test rights, permitted-decision lists, and the gap-validity conditions to axiom level in the architecture; rewrite the client framing to placement/matching language.
- **Non-reproducible AI scoring (io-selection-psych).** Change: amend Axiom 4 to "rigorous, reproducible, and auditable": frozen anchored codebooks, pinned re-runnable scorer versions, archived span-level rationales, published human dual-coding reliability; no consequential use of baseline metrics before dialect/language invariance evidence.
- **No form equivalence (test-publisher-veteran).** Item-level IRT is not the only route, but unconstrained generation is indefensible. Change: adaptation confined to a finite pretested bank of scenario templates with calibrated stake rungs and demonstrated within-family exchangeability; formative-only until exchangeability, coder-agreement, slope-reliability, and stake-invariance evidence exists.
- **De novo forfeits inheritable evidence (test-publisher-veteran).** The mapping would not transfer criterion validity, but the internal-only lineage memo is a concealment posture. Change: scope the de novo register to manuscript prose; publish the novelty watch as a construct crosswalk with convergent/discriminant predictions; bring chapters into compliance with the "say so plainly" rule.
- **Regulatory intersection (test-publisher-veteran).** Change: promote expected-objection 5 to a binding design constraint with a decision envelope, a compliance-artifact roadmap tied to the instrument deliverable, an acknowledged consent/disclosure obligation created by Axiom 5, and output-language rules for minor-facing reports.
- **PPRA and student-privacy statutes (edtech-buyer).** The theory does not require induced distress, but the channel as framed is near the SOPIPA paradigm case. Change: restructure the purchaser/channel (parent-consented outside school, or school-directed with DPA and no third-party disclosure); adopt PPRA consent as a design assumption under 18; add a legal register and a data lifecycle for the phrase-level audit trail; state that stakes are a scenario variable, not induced stress.
- **Motive labels as character verdicts (edtech-buyer).** The labels stay; the register does not. Change: add a "no motive carries moral valence" statement to the measures section; rewrite illustrations as time-indexed, behavior-anchored sketches; move report constraints (names never headline a minor's report, weights rendered in the respondent's own phrases, the seventeen line printed with every profile) into the binding specification.

## 4. Kill shots and how they fared

All nine were rebutted in their terminal conclusions and all nine partially stand on extracted concessions.

1. **Trait-psychometrician** (measurable part is redundant, novel part unmeasurable): partially stands. Forced the Conscientiousness resolution, a binding staged-validation ladder, and Revealed-tag downgrades; the redundancy claim fell to the stated-revealed and dissociation content.
2. **Dual-process-skeptic** (the binary is an output-format axiom the data cannot contradict): partially stands. Forced a stated retirement condition for the two-graph deliverable, demotion of the Kahneman lineage, and the fluency-confound bar; two elicited conditions survive under every rival processing account.
3. **Whole-trait-theorist** (thirty years old and unmeasurable at this density): partially stands. Forced lineage acknowledgment in the axioms, decontamination of the cross-system scoring rule, and slope gating; the ESM occasion-count constraint does not transfer to a designed stake grid.
4. **Developmental-psych** (slopes measure maturation; the business model voids the safety valve): partially stands. Forced a developmental-state register, reclassification of magnitude metrics as state-loaded, and conversion of the caveat into binding expiry-and-retest policy; the origin-agnostic ontology survived.
5. **Behavioral-economist** (there is no preference to reveal, only construction): partially stands. Forced deletion of the "finding about the person" sentence, ordinal re-scoping of slopes, a rewritten Axiom 5, and a construction-invariance validity gate; stable dispositions governing construction remain a live measurand.
6. **Metacognition-researcher** (no revealed channel exists for the flagship constructs): partially stands. Forced a binding elicitation discipline (cue manipulation, no description-format items), stated-only retagging of the attention axis pending that design, and a fluency invariance gate; work-product coding of deployed reasoning survived.
7. **Io-selection-psych** (the architecture structurally prevents validity evidence): partially stands. Forced the Revealed retag, a binding use restriction, gating of reactivity elicitation, and an explicit falsification condition on slope reliability; graceful degradation to baselines defeated the "cannot exist" conclusion.
8. **Test-publisher-veteran** (dies silently at the RFP stage): partially stands. Forced pre-committed reliability thresholds as a standing rule, a public construct crosswalk, and a deployment-staging commitment; "impossible in principle" was false (CAT, AIG, audited AI scoring are procurement-accepted).
9. **Edtech-buyer** (null intersection: everything novel is unprocurable, everything procurable is owned): partially stands. Forced a governance chapter, the stated-and-revealed-shown-together reporting rule, and a declaration that the conditional machinery is non-negotiable even if it forecloses K-12 district procurement; both horns of the dilemma were misread.

## 5. What survived cleanly

No objection fell outright, but the following maximal attacks were defeated inside the rulings and need no further defense:

- The "Big Five re-labeled" characterization: the conditional measurement objects (per-mode baselines plus slopes, mode divergence, stated-revealed gap) are not contained in any domain score, and the novelty watch disclaims rather than confesses taxonomic novelty.
- "Slopes are unrecoverable under any feasible administration": Cronbach-Furby condemns two-occasion observed differences, not latent slopes with partial pooling over a designed stake grid.
- The dual-process ontology charge: two elicited administration conditions exist and differ under continuum, unimodel, and accumulation accounts alike; the instrument never claimed a Type 1/Type 2 dissociation.
- The demand for lexical, factor-analytic, bottom-up derivation: within-person conditional parameters cannot in principle fall out of adjective-pool factor analysis, and Loevinger/Cronbach-Meehl license theory-first ordering.
- "The moderation claim is unfalsifiable": once reactivity is scored on its own scenarios, flat System 2 slopes in reactive people kill it outright.
- Sen's strict circularity: the two-channel definition, with the gap as a first-class metric, is not preference-defined-as-choice.
- Nisbett-Wilson as total defeat of the coding channel: structural properties of reasoning actually produced are behavior, not introspective report.
- "Attention targets are unmeasurable in principle": cue-manipulation designs, information search, and incidental memory are genuine revealed channels (they just are not yet written down).
- Karraker as per-se illegality: a normal-range, non-clinical stability measure is not a pre-offer medical exam by content.
- "Equating is impossible in principle": template-family calibration plus generalizability analysis is a Standards-recognized route for a typical-performance instrument.
- The faking/SJT-saturation transfer: with no effectiveness key and non-valenced poles, the mechanism that inflates keyed SJTs has no target.
- Ego-depletion collapse as fatal to System 1: the nulls concern a short-run causal willpower-stock manipulation, not individual differences in felt cost and recovery by source.
- The demand to rename Power and Triumph: euphemism decodes back to the same meaning; the fix is register and report constraints, not construct deletion.
- Every "this framework needs a reason to exist / should not exist / null intersection" terminal conclusion.

## 6. Questions to bring to the real second opinion

1. The panel converged on latent-slope estimation over a designed stake grid as the rescue for the headline metrics. What minimum design density (stake levels x items x modes x occasions) would you require before a per-person slope is reportable at all, and is that density achievable with adolescents in any administration we could actually run?
2. Is a preference-framed task-regulation system (effort allocation, follow-through, persistence) genuinely constructible without violating the preference-not-ability axiom, given that the trait literature operationalizes Conscientiousness as tendency, or does the axiom need a principled capacity clause?
3. Once population format effects, adolescent developmental miscalibration, and selection-incentive management are subtracted from the stated-revealed gap, what residual person variance do you expect remains, and is the metric worth its position as the framework's signature?
4. Should any slope be built before the described-stakes-engage-the-same-modulator assumption is tested against incentivized micro-stakes, and what calibration result would you accept as sufficient?
5. Positioned against Mischel-Shoda, Fleeson, and whole trait theory, is "a one-session, auditable parameterization of stake- and mode-conditioned reliance" a publishable novelty claim, or does the contribution reduce to instrumentation?
6. Can System 3 reactivity and System 2 stake-slopes ever be separated under a single AI-coded elicitation channel, or does the moderation claim have to wait for a full multitrait-multimethod design before it appears in the manuscript at all?
7. What decision envelope, if any, do you consider defensible for a normal-range preference instrument administered to minors under Messick's consequential-validity standard, and does the development/placement framing survive the fact that the first named client is evaluative?
8. If the fast and slow conditions fail to separate after a dense administration, the framework says it collapses to one graph plus a deliberation default. Is that residue still a contribution, and would you advise pre-registering that collapse as the primary study rather than the contingency?

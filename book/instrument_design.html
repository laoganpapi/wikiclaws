What changed. The point economy is gone; stakes are now review probability (a disclosed chance you explain your answer to the AI: none, 1-in-4, certain). Per-person stake-slopes are demoted to reliability-gated categories; baselines are the v1 headline. Two sittings instead of three. The ethics package is rebuilt for minors. Reactivity is estimated outside the stake manipulation. All cost figures are assumptions to validate, not quotes.

AThe Arena — thinking machine

15 scenarios (5 decision types × 3 review-probability tiers), tier order randomized F3. Split battery: 9 fast-only, 6 slow-only F13, plus 2 two-phase revision probes and 4 counterbalanced trap items F14.

Fast items

  • Unlimited reading; 5-second clock starts at choice onset F10
  • Concurrent load (hold a 4-digit code) on half the items
  • Forced binary probe under the clock: one free peek, data card vs principle card F11
  • One-liner rationale coded as stated-side data; wording varies per item, near-duplicates flagged F12

Slow items

  • Up to 4 minutes; shelf cards free but budgeted (3 non-bankable opens; dwell + scroll telemetry) F5
  • Tools scored on engagement, not opening: tool output must be congruent with the chosen option F25
  • Behavioral and text channels corroborate only if conditionally independent

Scoring. Per mode and axis: an IRT-style baseline plus a categorical stake response (rises / flat / falls) tested against a permutation null F15. Mode divergence disattenuated. A per-person stake-salience check gates interpretation of flat profiles F2 F17.

BThe Ledger — social energy

  • One-day DRM (yesterday), honest 15-minute budget F28; day two and EMA move to phase 2
  • Portfolio inventory: countable recall, unchanged
  • Risk appetite: review election (free, no fee F21), two message drafts, challenge election; scored only on convergence of 3+ indicators, with election-specific invariance tests and a pre-registered drop rule F23
  • Stated battery: asynchronous, after both sittings

CThe Room — disposition

  • 6 SJT vignettes; in two, a free probe budget splits between check-your-own-reaction and read-the-field: the behavioral attention-target indicator F18
  • One dispute + one friction probe, disclosed in specific assent, debriefed, circuit-breakered F22 F21
  • Probe-window behavior reported as a lower bound on reactivity F26
  • Reactivity = friction recovery curve + stated stability only; moderation of S2 slopes becomes a testable cross-loading F17 F31

DThe Compass — objectives

  • Laddered open prompt, unchanged
  • 16 tradeoff pairs; motive contrasts disguised in scenario texture with fillers and rotated framings; cross-framing inconsistency is the integrity signal F27
  • The spend: one choice among five report-deliverable options; no points; hardest-truths page adult-only F32 F22

§Use policy (binding)

Finding 20 paid in full

  1. Developmental feedback only, until criterion validity exists
  2. Reactivity, stability, and gap metrics never released for selection use
  3. Respondent controls release; minors' scores expire after 18 months
  4. Minors' gaps: system level only, above a reliability floor, framed symmetrically; never in third-party reports F24
  5. Ethics reader signs off on this policy before any minor cohort

⊕Validation (rescoped, n = 300)

  • Mode elicitation checked against Type-1 markers (load-task interference, probe choices), not latency compression F10
  • Discriminant vs ICAR-16 on baselines only, |r| < .20, reliability-corrected, multiplicity-corrected F29
  • MTMM model with a coded-prose method factor for S2-sourcing vs S3-attention F18
  • Retest on parallel Form B at 4–6 weeks; slope categories below r = .4 revert to cohort-level F19
  • AI–human ICC ≥ .75; fairness adds precision-by-language-group and election invariance F33 F23
  • Attrition analysis pre-registered; dropout is endogenous to the constructs F30

# A New Theory of Personality

A working paper, with Steven Pham, that sets out the framework for an AI-enabled personality
assessment. The assessment is intended for high school students, college students, and young
people entering the workforce, and the framework is written to be defensible to an academic or
cognitive-science reviewer.

The theory holds that personality is a set of preferences, stated or revealed, that govern how a
person makes decisions and responds to stimuli, measured at a time and in service of pursuing an
external cause. It is built from separable systems, presented to a uniform template, with the set
of systems left open.

## Manuscript

- `00_architecture.md` — working spine: decisions, design axioms, the system template, the system
  register, cross-system links, and the objections we expect from a reviewer. Not part of the
  working paper; read it first.
- `01_introduction.md` — Chapter 1. What the theory measures and why; the definition; what the
  theory is for.
- `02_social_energy.md` — Chapter 2. The social energy economy (energy gain, game choice, risk
  appetite).
- `03_thinking_machine.md` — Chapter 3. The thinking machine (internal/external cognitive sourcing
  and formal/informal method, fast/slow as elicited modes, stakes as modulator; output is two
  graphs of reliance, not a strength score). The centerpiece.
- `04_emotional_social_disposition.md` — Chapter 4. Emotional and social disposition: stated or
  revealed preferences and tendencies in emotional and social situations, not competence
  (agreeableness/confrontation, introspection/situational awareness, and emotional stability, which
  subsumes neuroticism and moderates the other systems' stake responses).
- `05_objectives.md` — Chapter 5. Objectives (open-prompt motivation coded to a five-motive
  profile).

## Status

Four systems adopted and drafted. The framework is at the working-paper stage. No critique or
development harness has been run; the next natural use of one is an adversarial pass that
simulates the academic second opinion (dual-process skeptic, psychometrician on discriminant
validity, fairness reviewer) before that opinion is sought for real.

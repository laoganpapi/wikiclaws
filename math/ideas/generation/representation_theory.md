## PART I — COLLATZ

### I.1 The group action and isotypic decomposition

`U_n := (Z/3^nZ)^× ` is **cyclic** of order `φ(3^n) = 2·3^{n-1}`, generated by `2`
(verified: `ord_{3^n}(2) = φ(3^n)` for all tested `n`). The Syracuse step is

```
    x  ↦  2^{-k}(3x + 1)   (mod 3^n),    k ~ Geom(2),    x ∈ U_n.
```

This is **affine, not multiplicative**: a multiplication by the unit `2^{-k}` (which,
in the cyclic log-coordinate `x = 2^a`, is just a *translation* `a ↦ a−k`) **followed by
the additive shift `+1`** coming from `3x+1`. The smallest group seeing *both* operations
is the **affine group**

```
    G_n  :=  Aff(Z/3^n)  =  (Z/3^n, +) ⋊ (Z/3^n)^× .
```

Its irreducibles, by the **Mackey little-group / Gelfand-pair machine** over the additive
characters `ψ_t(x) = e^{2πi t x / 3^n}`, are:
- the `φ(3^n)` one-dimensional characters of `U_n` (trivial on translations) — **these are
  exactly the abelian Fourier modes that the prior `(Z/3^n)^×`-attack used, and they failed**;
- for each `U_n`-orbit of non-trivial additive characters `ψ_t`, one **induced** irrep of
  dimension `= |orbit|`. Orbits are graded by the 3-adic valuation `v_3(t)`:
  `v_3(t)=n` (`t=0`, trivial), `v_3=n−1` (the **mod-3 characters**, the obstruction),
  …, `v_3=0` (`t` a unit, the **generic** orbit of size `φ(3^n)` — the big non-abelian block).

**The correct invariant filtration** (the one `P_n` actually respects) is the dual /
induced-from-trivial tower
```
    V^(1) ⊂ V^(2) ⊂ … ⊂ V^(n) = C^{U_n},   V^(k) = span{ 1[x ≡ r (mod 3^k)] }_r,
    dim V^(k) = φ(3^k),
```
i.e. functions constant on cosets `U_n / U_n^{(k)}`, `U_n^{(k)} = ker(U_n → (Z/3^k)^×)`.
By `transfer_operator.md §3.1` (re-verified exactly here, leak `≤ 3·10^{-16}`) **each `V^(k)`
is `P_n`-invariant**, and `P_n|_{V^(k)} = P_k`. The successive quotients
`V^(k)/V^(k−1)` are the genuinely-new isotypic pieces, of dimension
`φ(3^k) − φ(3^{k−1}) = 4·3^{k−2}` (`= 2,4,12,36,…`).

### I.2 Why this escapes the barrier — and the numerical payoff

The proven obstruction (`perp_gap.md`) is: `π_n` has permanent mod-3 marginal `(1/3,2/3)`,
`E_n → ∞`, and the obstruction is the `λ=1` eigenvector `π_n|_V` living in the **bottom block
`V^(1)`**. The abelian-Fourier attack failed because the multiplicative characters only
diagonalize the `2^{-k}` part; they are **blind to the `+1`**, which is what couples the
isotypes. Concretely (`collatz_isotypic.py`, decomposition (A)):

> **Multiplicative-equivariance defect** `‖P_n Π − Π P_n‖` (Π = multiply-by-2 permutation):
> `0.67, 1.59, 3.23, 5.87` for `n=1..4` — **growing, strictly `>0`**: `P_n` is *not*
> `U_n`-equivariant, so no abelian-Fourier basis can diagonalize it. This is the precise
> reason the `(Z/3^n)^×`-character attack was doomed.

The **non-abelian** induced decomposition (D) localizes the obstruction completely:

```
   n=4  induced filtration (block radius = on V^(k);  quotient radius = on V^(k)/V^(k-1)):
     V^(1) dim  2   block_radius 1.0000 | quotient dim  2  spec_radius 1.0000   <- mod-3 isotype: carries λ=1
     V^(2) dim  6   block_radius 1.0000 | quotient dim  4  spec_radius 0.0000   <- NILPOTENT
     V^(3) dim 18   block_radius 1.0000 | quotient dim 12  spec_radius 0.0000   <- NILPOTENT
     V^(4) dim 54   block_radius 1.0000 | quotient dim 36  spec_radius 0.0000   <- NILPOTENT
```

(identical pattern for `n=2,3`; leaks `~10^{-16}`, confirming exact invariance).

> **Finding (the escape, made concrete).** The eigenvalue `1` — the *entire* natural-density
> obstruction — lives **only in the bottom (mod-3, trivial-on-`U_n/U_n^{(1)}`) isotype
> `V^(1)`**. **Every successive non-trivial isotypic quotient `V^(k)/V^(k−1)`, `k≥2`, is
> nilpotent (spectral radius exactly `0`).** Decomposition (B) confirms the same from the
> dual side: the **generic block** (unit-frequency additive characters, dim `36` at `n=4`)
> has **spectral radius `0`** for all `n≥2`.

This is a *representation-theoretic refinement* of the `perp_gap.md` result
(`χ_{P_n} = λ^{φ−1}(λ−1)`): not only is `P_n−Π` nilpotent, but the nilpotency is **graded by
the induced isotypes**, with the obstruction confined to the single 2-dimensional bottom
isotype and the `O(3^n)`-dimensional complement carrying *zero* spectral content.

### I.3 The direction (what to do with it)

The barrier in `perp_gap.md` was: spectral radius `0` on the complement is **not** norm
contraction (`‖P_n−Π‖_2 > 1`, nilpotency index `= n → ∞`). The representation-theoretic
move that the graded nilpotency suggests:

1. **Search for an `n`-uniform norm in which each quotient `V^(k)/V^(k−1)` contracts.**
   Because the quotients are *irreducible-isotypic* pieces of an **induced** rep
   `Ind_{U_n^{(k)}}^{U_n} 1`, Frobenius reciprocity + the **wreath-recursion** of the tower
   (`V^(k)` is `P_k` lifted) means the quotient operators are *self-similar copies of one
   another* (branch-group structure — cf. Grigorchuk/Bartholdi-Grigorchuk self-similar
   operators on rooted trees). A norm adapted to the self-similar contraction (a Bartholdi
   "contracting"-style weight on tree levels) could turn "nilpotent index `n`, norm `>1`"
   into "`n`-uniform geometric contraction *off the bottom isotype*". That is precisely the
   missing quantitative ingredient `perp_gap.md §4.3` identified as absent.

2. **Attack only the bottom isotype `V^(1)` separately.** The whole problem is now a
   *2-dimensional* fixed obstruction `(1/3,2/3)` plus a self-similar nilpotent tail. The
   honest reframing: Collatz-natural-density ⟺ controlling the **interaction of the bottom
   isotype with the contracting tail across `n`** — an explicit, low-dimensional, group-graded
   target, not an opaque `φ(3^n)`-dim mixing problem.

**Name-check:** Mackey little-group method; Frobenius reciprocity / induced reps;
Gelfand pairs (`(Aff(Z/3^n), U_n)` is a candidate Gelfand pair — worth verifying, it would
force multiplicity-freeness and make the isotypic blocks 1-dimensional, simplifying (1));
branch-group / self-similar-operator theory (Grigorchuk, Bartholdi-Grigorchuk-Šunić) for the
contracting-norm step; Peter–Weyl/Schur for the block-diagonalization.

### I.4 Collatz validation (done)

- Exact-invariance check: induced filtration leaks `≤ 3·10^{-16}` (`n≤4`). ✔
- Obstruction localization: `λ=1` in `V^(1)` only; quotients `V^(k)/V^(k−1)` spectral
  radius `0` (`k≥2`). ✔ Cross-checks the exact `χ_{P_n}=λ^{φ−1}(λ−1)` of `perp_gap.md`.
- Abelian-failure diagnosis: multiplicative-equivariance defect `>0` and growing. ✔

### I.5 Collatz plausibility, failure modes

**Plausibility: 2.5 / 5.** Honest. The decomposition is *correct and clean* and gives the
**sharpest structural statement of the obstruction yet** (graded nilpotency; obstruction =
one 2-d isotype). But:
- **Failure mode A (the known wall reappears).** The graded picture does **not** by itself
  supply the `n`-uniform *norm* contraction `perp_gap.md §4.3` proved is missing. Step I.3(1)

## PART II — FRANKL

### II.1 The group action and isotypic decomposition

For a union-closed `F` with lattice `L=(F,∪)`, the automorphism group `G := Aut(L)` acts on
the ground set `[n]` (a lattice auto permutes the join-irreducibles, hence the ground
elements). It therefore permutes fibres, `Fib(g·x) = g·Fib(x)`, so:

- the **frequency vector** `v = (|Fib(x)|/|L|)_x` is `G`-**invariant** — it lies in the
  **trivial isotype** of the permutation module `C^{[n]}`;
- the **second-order fibre-overlap matrix** `M[x,y] = |Fib(x) ∩ Fib(y)|/|L|` is
  `G`-**equivariant** (`PM = MP` for every `P ∈ ρ(G)`, verified, defect `< 10^{-14}`).

By Maschke/Peter–Weyl the permutation module splits
```
    C^{[n]}  =  (trivial isotype)  ⊕  (non-trivial isotypes),
```
the trivial part being `fix(G)` (dimension = number of `G`-orbits on `[n]`; we project onto
it with the **Reynolds operator** `Π_triv = |G|^{-1} Σ_g ρ(g)`). Since `M` commutes with `G`,
**`M` is block-diagonal across isotypes**, and we may read the spectrum of `M` on the
trivial block and on the non-trivial complement separately.

### II.2 Why this escapes the barrier

The proven barrier: *every `S_n`-symmetric convex moment of `v` is flat-minimized by the cube
at abundance `1/2`*. A symmetric convex moment `Σ_x φ(v_x)` factors through the
`S_n`-orbit-average of `v`, i.e. through the **`S_n`-trivial (constant) component** — the mean
`⟨v,1⟩/n`, which the cube pins at exactly `1/2` with zero margin (`lp_duality.md §4`). **The
non-trivial `Aut(L)`-isotypes are orthogonal to every symmetric moment by construction**, so
the "flat at the cube" obstruction — a statement about the trivial component — does not bind
them. Likewise the failed Boolean-Fourier attack used *abelian* `(Z/2)^n` characters whose
quadratic statistics are flat on the cube; here the relevant group is the **non-abelian
`Aut(L)`**, and the object is the *equivariant operator `M`*, not a scalar moment.

### II.3 The numerical payoff (the separator)

`frankl_isotypic.py` over 467 small UC families (`n ≤ 4`):

> **On the Boolean cube `B_k`** (the symmetric extremizer): the **non-trivial-isotype block
> of `M` is degenerate** — a single repeated eigenvalue:
> `B_2: [0.25]`, `B_3: [0.25, 0.25]` (i.e. all `= (1/2)^2`), and the frequency vector's
> non-trivial component is **exactly `0`** (norm `~10^{-16}`). *All* abundance information on
> the cube sits in the **trivial isotype**, and the non-trivial isotypes are **flat**.

> **On non-cube lattices** the non-trivial-isotype spectrum of `M` **spreads**: e.g. the
> `|L|=7`, `Aut=Z/2`, abundance `0.714` family has non-trivial-block spectrum
> `[0.0546, 0.374]` (spread `0.319`); generic non-cube families show comparable spreads.

So there is a concrete **non-symmetric, `Aut(L)`-equivariant functional** —
"the spectral spread of `M` on the non-trivial isotypes" — that is **flat (degenerate)
exactly on the symmetric extremizers** (cube and the chain/diagonal degenerate lattices) and
**non-degenerate on the lattices with abundance bounded away from `1/2`**. This is precisely
the *non-symmetric ingredient* the barrier said was necessary: the symmetric moments cannot
see it, yet it tracks the gap `abundance − 1/2`.

### II.4 The direction (what to do with it)

Conjecture to test/build toward (`[NOVELTY UNVERIFIED]`):

> **(F-iso)** There is an `Aut(L)`-equivariant, isotypically-supported functional `Φ(M)` —
> e.g. `Φ = ‖Π_nontriv M Π_nontriv‖` rescaled, or the top non-trivial-isotype eigenvalue
> minus its flat-cube value `1/4` — such that `abundance(F) ≥ 1/2 + c·Φ(M)` with `Φ(M)=0`
> **iff** `L` is a Boolean cube. Combined with the proven cube-uniqueness extremizer result
> (`join_irreducible_labelling.md §5`, the cube is the *unique* `minab=1/2` lattice), this
> would give a Frankl proof route that is *non-symmetric by design*.

The lever is the **reconstruction identity** `Fib(x) = ⋃_{j∈JI, x∈j} ↑j` (`R`): it makes `M`
a *union-of-filters Gram matrix*, and its non-trivial-isotype block encodes the **overlaps
between JI-filters sharing the top `T`** — exactly the union-overlap structure
`join_irreducible_labelling.md §6` flagged as where abundance lives and which the
first-moment (trivial-isotype) identity discards.

**Name-check:** Maschke/Peter–Weyl (isotypic decomposition); Reynolds operator (group
averaging); Schur's lemma (equivariant `M` is scalar on each isotype copy — this is *why* the
cube's non-trivial block is a single repeated eigenvalue, since `B_k`'s permutation rep is
`trivial ⊕ standard` with the standard part irreducible, forcing `M` scalar there);
representation theory of `S_n` and of the hyperoctahedral group `B_k = Aut(B_k)`.

### II.5 Frankl validation (done)

- `M`-equivariance under `Aut(L)`: defect `< 10^{-14}` on all families. ✔
- Cube degeneracy: non-trivial-isotype block of `M` is a single repeated eigenvalue
  `(1/2)^2`, frequency non-trivial component `= 0`. ✔ (Schur's lemma prediction.)
- Separation: non-cube lattices with abundance `> 1/2` have non-degenerate non-trivial
  spectrum (spread up to `0.32`). ✔

### II.6 Frankl plausibility, failure modes

**Plausibility: 2 / 5.** A real non-symmetric signal that tracks the gap — but:
- **Failure mode A (the separator is not clean at `1/2`).** Chain/diagonal degenerate
  lattices (e.g. `{∅,[k]}`) *also* have flat non-trivial blocks at abundance `1/2` without
  being cubes (numerics §II.3). So `Φ(M)=0 ⇔ cube` is **false as stated**; the true
  zero-set is "symmetric-extremal lattices", a larger family. (F-iso) needs repair — perhaps
  `Φ` must combine the isotypic block with the JI-union structure of `(R)`. This is the
  central honest gap.
- **Failure mode B (`Aut(L)` is often trivial).** Most UC families have `Aut(L) = 1`, so the
  decomposition is vacuous (`C^{[n]}` is all "trivial isotype") and `Φ ≡` a symmetric moment.
  The escape only has teeth for *symmetric* lattices — which include the extremizers, the case
  that matters, but a proof must handle asymmetric `L` by **another mechanism** (e.g.
  `Aut`-breaking induction, or averaging over an `S_n`-orbit of labellings rather than over
  `Aut(L)`).
- **Failure mode C (no `c>0`).** Even with a clean `Φ`, the inequality
  `abundance ≥ 1/2 + cΦ` may fail or need `c→0` as `n→∞`, recreating the margin problem.
- **Prior art.** Equivariant/isotypic analysis of association-scheme-like Gram matrices on
  lattices is classical (Bose–Mesner algebras, Terwilliger algebra of the cube). The cube's
  `M` being scalar on the standard isotype is a Bose–Mesner fact. `[NOVELTY UNVERIFIED]`;
  prior is HIGH that the *cube* computation is folklore, LOWER that the *separator across all
  UC lattices* has been posed.
